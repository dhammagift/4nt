Footnotes ▾ Footnote
☢️ Warning: Sujato's views on Jhāna contradict the Early Buddhist Texts (EBT). His interpretation relies on the Visuddhimagga — a Theravāda commentary written roughly 500 years after the EBT — rather than the source texts themselves. His translations are widely used but his doctrinal views should not be taken at face value. Always verify with reputable EBT sources. Read his translations and notes with great care and wise discrimination.
